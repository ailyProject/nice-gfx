# Additional bundled bitmap fonts

These fonts may be used for free in commercial products. Keep their copyright
and license notices with redistributions. The editor and exported firmware use
the same 95 printable ASCII glyphs (U+0020–U+007E), including a `?` fallback.
All characters, including spaces and punctuation, occupy the same fixed cell.
Chinese glyphs are not included.

| Object | Cell | Baseline | Bitmap bytes | License |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `Scientifica5x11` | 5 × 11 | 9 | 1,045 | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| `Tamzen7x14` | 7 × 14 | 11 | 1,330 | Tamzen/Tamsyn permissive license |
| `Spleen8x16` | 8 × 16 | 12 | 1,520 | BSD 2-Clause |

Sources:

- [Scientifica v2.3](https://github.com/oppiliappan/scientifica/releases/tag/v2.3),
  by Akshay Oppiliappan, based on Creep by Romeo Van Snick. Original glyph
  coordinates are retained in 5 × 11 cells; the larger global BDF bounds apply
  to non-ASCII glyphs. Font data remains under OFL 1.1 and may be bundled with
  commercial software, but may not be sold on its own. Retain
  `Scientifica-OFL-1.1.txt` and the upstream `Creep-MIT.txt` notice.
- [Tamzen](https://github.com/sunaku/tamzen-font), by Suraj N. Kurapati, based on
  Tamsyn by Scott Fial. The complete license for both authors is in `Tamzen.txt`.
- [Spleen](https://github.com/fcambus/spleen), by Frederic Cambus. Retain
  `Spleen-BSD-2-Clause.txt` in source and binary distributions.

The unchanged upstream BDF sources live only in
[`nice_gfx_editor/scripts/font-sources`](../../../nice_gfx_editor/scripts/font-sources/README.md). `sources.json` pins
their URLs, commits/release, SHA-256 hashes and complete license notices.
Conversion only subsets ASCII and repacks the bitmaps; it does not rescale or
redraw glyphs. No font software is downloaded or executed by the application.

The committed C++ font units are the firmware build inputs. After updating them, run `node scripts/rebuild-runtime.mjs` from the editor repository to refresh the runtime archive and its integrity manifest. Font rasterization tools are maintained separately from the firmware library.
Browser licenses are in `public/licenses/`; exported Arduino licenses are in
`src/nice_gfx/Fonts/licenses/`. Only referenced font data needs to be linked.

The larger `Spleen12x24` and bold `TamzenBold8x16` are generated by the same
offline tool from additional pinned BDFs, using the same upstream licenses.
Their metrics and the four extended Chinese faces are documented in
[ExtendedDeviceFonts.md](ExtendedDeviceFonts.md).
