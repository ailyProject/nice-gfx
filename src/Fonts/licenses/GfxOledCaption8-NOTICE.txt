SIL OFL 1.1; retain RussoOne-OFL.txt. The derivative name does not use the Reserved Font Name Russo.
Cell 6x9, baseline 7, cap height 7, 96 glyphs (ASCII + degree).
Rasterization: source outlines at 4x coverage; area-average threshold 180/255, row-major MSB-first 1bpp. Wide outlines are width-constrained to fixed cells; tall ASCII descenders are fitted within the cell.
Percent scale 1, degree scale 0.55; numeric symbols align at the baseline. Period/comma/colon ink is inside the compressed width/3 advance; space advances width/2 for numeric faces. The W glyph receives a native 5x7 grid hint so its two counters remain visible. No screenshot glyph extraction or operating-system font fallback.
Rebuild: node scripts/ssd1306-projects/fonts/generate.mjs <optional Playwright module path>.
