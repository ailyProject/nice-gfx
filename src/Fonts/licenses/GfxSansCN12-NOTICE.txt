GfxSansCN12 is a modified bitmap font derived from Source Han Sans CN Bold 2.005R.
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans/6c709ca72d3d7c46ab42ebecc1a26e7d69595a37/SubsetOTF/CN/SourceHanSansCN-Bold.otf

License: SIL OFL 1.1; retain SourceHanSans-OFL-1.1.txt with this notice.
Modified by NICE_GFX: actual 12px outline rasterization into 12x16 monochrome cells,
GB2312 + ASCII subsetting and fixed advance. Box drawing symbols are rasterized at
11px to retain their overhanging strokes inside the cell; no bitmap resizing.
The derived name does not use upstream Reserved Font Names.
