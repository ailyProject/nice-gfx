Cell 6x12, baseline 9, descent 3, 95 glyphs U+0020-U+007E.
The BDF advance and baseline coordinates are preserved. No outline rasterization, coverage threshold, resampling, horizontal fit, synthetic bold, or per-glyph redraw.
Native size is 12px; optional larger previews replicate pixels by integer nearest-neighbor only.
ASCII only: Chinese text requires a separate native CJK font and matching line metrics.
Rebuild offline: node scripts/package-pixel-fonts.mjs
